Pasta Salad
Boil pasta until soft. Chop tomatoes and lettuce. If you want it spicy, add pepper. Mix everything in a bowl. If it looks dry, add olive oil. Serve cold.

Cheese Omelette
Beat eggs in a bowl. Add cheese and a pinch of salt. Heat frying pan, pour mixture. If it sticks, add more oil. Flip when edges are brown. Serve hot.

Chicken Stir Fry
Cut chicken breast into strips. Slice vegetables. Heat oil in a pan. Add chicken, cook until white. Add vegetables, stir for 5 minutes. If too dry, add water. Serve with rice.

Simple Rice
Wash rice. Boil in pot with water. If water evaporates before rice is soft, add more water. Fluff with fork and serve.

Vegetarian Chili
Saute onion and bell pepper. Add kidney beans and tomatoes. Stir in chili powder. Simmer for 20 minutes. Serve hot.

Greek Salad
Chop cucumber and tomatoes. Add feta cheese and olives. Drizzle with olive oil. Toss and serve.

Beef Tacos
Cook ground beef in a pan. Fill taco shells with beef, lettuce, cheese, and salsa. Serve immediately.

Vegan Buddha Bowl
Cook quinoa. Slice carrot and avocado. Add chickpeas and spinach. Arrange in a bowl and serve.

Fish and Chips
Coat fish fillets in flour. Fry in hot oil until golden. Slice potatoes, fry until crisp. Serve together.

Egg Fried Rice
Scramble eggs in a pan. Add cooked rice, carrot, peas, and soy sauce. Stir-fry for 5 minutes.

Shakshuka
Saute onion and bell pepper. Add tomatoes and simmer. Make wells, crack eggs in, cook until set.

Lentil Soup
Boil lentils with carrot, onion, celery, and water. Simmer until soft. Blend if desired. Serve warm.

Caprese Salad
Slice tomatoes and mozzarella. Layer with basil. Drizzle with olive oil. Serve fresh.

Tofu Stir Fry
Cube tofu, fry until golden. Add broccoli, carrot, and soy sauce. Stir-fry for 5 minutes.

Classic BLT
Fry bacon. Layer bread with bacon, lettuce, and tomato. Serve as a sandwich.

Vegetable Curry
Saute potato, carrot, and peas. Add curry powder and coconut milk. Simmer until vegetables are soft.

Eggplant Parmesan
Slice eggplant, bake until soft. Layer with tomato sauce and cheese. Bake until golden.

Falafel Wrap
Warm falafel and tortilla. Add lettuce, tomato, and yogurt. Wrap and serve.

Mushroom Risotto
Saute onion and mushroom. Add rice, cook with broth. Stir in parmesan and butter at the end.

Chicken Caesar Salad
Grill chicken. Toss lettuce with parmesan, croutons, and dressing. Top with sliced chicken.

Tomato Soup
Simmer tomatoes, onion, and garlic in water. Blend until smooth. Serve hot.

Tuna Sandwich
Mix tuna with mayonnaise. Spread on bread with lettuce. Serve as a sandwich.

Vegetable Stir Fry
Stir-fry broccoli, carrot, and bell pepper. Add soy sauce. Serve hot.

Quiche Lorraine
Mix eggs, bacon, cheese, and milk. Pour into pie crust. Bake until set.

Minestrone Soup
Boil carrot, celery, onion, tomato, pasta, beans, and water. Simmer until vegetables are soft.

Avocado Toast
Mash avocado with lemon juice. Spread on toasted bread. Serve immediately.

Peanut Butter Banana Sandwich
Spread peanut butter on bread. Add banana slices. Top with another slice of bread.

Vegetable Quesadilla
Fill tortilla with cheese, bell pepper, and onion. Cook on pan until cheese melts. Fold and serve.

Chickpea Stew
Saute onion, carrot, and tomato. Add chickpeas and spices. Simmer until thickened.

Egg Drop Soup
Boil chicken broth. Stir in cornstarch. Slowly pour in beaten eggs while stirring. Garnish with spring onion.

Sweet Potato Fries
Slice sweet potatoes. Toss with oil and salt. Bake until crispy.

Spinach Frittata
Beat eggs with spinach, cheese, and milk. Pour into pan. Cook until set.

Couscous Salad
Prepare couscous. Mix with chopped cucumber, tomato, and olive oil. Serve cold.

Turkey Wrap
Fill tortilla with turkey, lettuce, and cheese. Roll up and serve.

Pumpkin Soup
Boil pumpkin, onion, and carrot in water. Blend until smooth. Serve hot.

Salmon Teriyaki
Pan-fry salmon fillets. Add soy sauce. Serve with rice and broccoli.

Potato Gratin
Layer sliced potatoes with cheese, milk, and butter. Bake until golden.

Stuffed Peppers
Fill bell peppers with rice, ground beef, and tomato sauce. Bake until peppers are soft.

Pasta Primavera
Boil pasta. Saute zucchini, carrot, and peas. Mix with pasta and serve.

French Toast
Dip bread in egg, milk, and sugar. Fry until golden on both sides.

Vegetable Paella
Cook rice with peas, bell pepper, tomato, and saffron. Simmer until rice is tender.

Chicken Fajitas
Saute chicken, bell pepper, and onion. Serve in tortillas.

Miso Soup
Dissolve miso paste in water. Add tofu and spring onion. Heat gently and serve.
