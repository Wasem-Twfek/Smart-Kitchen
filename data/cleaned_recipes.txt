pasta salad boil pasta until soft. chop tomatoes and lettuce. if you want it spicy, add pepper. mix everything in a bowl. if it looks dry, add olive oil. serve cold. cheese omelette beat eggs in a bowl. add cheese and a pinch of salt. heat frying pan, pour mixture. if it sticks, add more oil. flip when edges are brown. serve hot. chicken stir fry cut chicken breast into strips. slice vegetables. heat oil in a pan. add chicken, cook until white. add vegetables, stir for 5 minutes. if too dry, add water. serve with rice. simple rice wash rice. boil in pot with water. if water evaporates before rice is soft, add more water. fluff with fork and serve. vegetarian chili saute onion and bell pepper. add kidney beans and tomatoes. stir in chili powder. simmer for 20 minutes. serve hot. greek salad chop cucumber and tomatoes. add feta cheese and olives. drizzle with olive oil. toss and serve. beef tacos cook ground beef in a pan. fill taco shells with beef, lettuce, cheese, and salsa. serve immediately. vegan buddha bowl cook quinoa. slice carrot and avocado. add chickpeas and spinach. arrange in a bowl and serve. fish and chips coat fish fillets in flour. fry in hot oil until golden. slice potatoes, fry until crisp. serve together. egg fried rice scramble eggs in a pan. add cooked rice, carrot, peas, and soy sauce. stirfry for 5 minutes. shakshuka saute onion and bell pepper. add tomatoes and simmer. make wells, crack eggs in, cook until set. lentil soup boil lentils with carrot, onion, celery, and water. simmer until soft. blend if desired. serve warm. caprese salad slice tomatoes and mozzarella. layer with basil. drizzle with olive oil. serve fresh. tofu stir fry cube tofu, fry until golden. add broccoli, carrot, and soy sauce. stirfry for 5 minutes. classic blt fry bacon. layer bread with bacon, lettuce, and tomato. serve as a sandwich. vegetable curry saute potato, carrot, and peas. add curry powder and coconut milk. simmer until vegetables are soft. eggplant parmesan slice eggplant, bake until soft. layer with tomato sauce and cheese. bake until golden. falafel wrap warm falafel and tortilla. add lettuce, tomato, and yogurt. wrap and serve. mushroom risotto saute onion and mushroom. add rice, cook with broth. stir in parmesan and butter at the end. chicken caesar salad grill chicken. toss lettuce with parmesan, croutons, and dressing. top with sliced chicken. tomato soup simmer tomatoes, onion, and garlic in water. blend until smooth. serve hot. tuna sandwich mix tuna with mayonnaise. spread on bread with lettuce. serve as a sandwich. vegetable stir fry stirfry broccoli, carrot, and bell pepper. add soy sauce. serve hot. quiche lorraine mix eggs, bacon, cheese, and milk. pour into pie crust. bake until set. minestrone soup boil carrot, celery, onion, tomato, pasta, beans, and water. simmer until vegetables are soft. avocado toast mash avocado with lemon juice. spread on toasted bread. serve immediately. peanut butter banana sandwich spread peanut butter on bread. add banana slices. top with another slice of bread. vegetable quesadilla fill tortilla with cheese, bell pepper, and onion. cook on pan until cheese melts. fold and serve. chickpea stew saute onion, carrot, and tomato. add chickpeas and spices. simmer until thickened. egg drop soup boil chicken broth. stir in cornstarch. slowly pour in beaten eggs while stirring. garnish with spring onion. sweet potato fries slice sweet potatoes. toss with oil and salt. bake until crispy. spinach frittata beat eggs with spinach, cheese, and milk. pour into pan. cook until set. couscous salad prepare couscous. mix with chopped cucumber, tomato, and olive oil. serve cold. turkey wrap fill tortilla with turkey, lettuce, and cheese. roll up and serve. pumpkin soup boil pumpkin, onion, and carrot in water. blend until smooth. serve hot. salmon teriyaki panfry salmon fillets. add soy sauce. serve with rice and broccoli. potato gratin layer sliced potatoes with cheese, milk, and butter. bake until golden. stuffed peppers fill bell peppers with rice, ground beef, and tomato sauce. bake until peppers are soft. pasta primavera boil pasta. saute zucchini, carrot, and peas. mix with pasta and serve. french toast dip bread in egg, milk, and sugar. fry until golden on both sides. vegetable paella cook rice with peas, bell pepper, tomato, and saffron. simmer until rice is tender. chicken fajitas saute chicken, bell pepper, and onion. serve in tortillas. miso soup dissolve miso paste in water. add tofu and spring onion. heat gently and serve.
